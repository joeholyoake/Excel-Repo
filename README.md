# Excel Data Analysis Project

## Overview

This project was completed as part of a **Data Technician bootcamp**, with a focus on developing practical Microsoft Excel skills for analysing and presenting data. The workbook includes tasks using retail sales, student grades, bike sales, and product sales datasets, covering data preparation, analysis, PivotTables, formulas, and visualisations.

## Skills Demonstrated

### Formulas and Functions

I used a range of Excel formulas to calculate, analyse, and extract insights from datasets, including:

* `SUM` — calculating total values, such as total commission
* `SUMIF` and `SUMIFS` — calculating totals based on specific criteria, including sales by gender and category
* `AVERAGE` — calculating average values
* `AVERAGEIF` — comparing average quantities based on criteria
* `DATE`, `MONTH`, and `YEAR` — working with and extracting information from date data
* `UNIQUE` — identifying distinct values within a dataset
* `VLOOKUP` — retrieving related information from data tables
* `SWITCH` — categorising sales volumes into **High**, **Medium**, and **Low** groups

The project specifically used conditional formulas to investigate sales patterns by gender and category, as well as the `SWITCH` function to classify product sales volumes.

### Data Cleaning and Organisation

* Converted raw datasets into structured Excel tables using `Ctrl + T`
* Checked and corrected data types
* Identified and removed hidden spaces from numerical data
* Applied filters to focus on relevant records
* Sorted data to identify highest and lowest values
* Organised datasets to make analysis more efficient

### PivotTables

Created and used PivotTables to summarise and explore data across multiple categories, including:

* Sales by country and market
* Profitability by country, age group, and gender
* Sales performance by county and product
* Comparisons between different market segments
* Sorting PivotTable results to identify the most profitable groups

The analysis found, for example, that the United States was the most profitable country, adults aged 35–64 were the most profitable age group, and females were the most profitable gender group in the relevant bike sales analysis.

### Charts and Data Visualisation

* Created charts and PivotCharts from existing PivotTables
* Used visualisations to communicate sales and business insights
* Explored how graphs and tables can make findings easier to understand
* Developed experience preparing data for presentation to stakeholders

The bootcamp work also focused on presenting analysis through clear tables, graphs, and visual summaries.

## Key Learning Outcomes

This project strengthened my ability to work with raw data and turn it into meaningful insights. I developed practical experience in using Excel formulas, filtering and sorting records, creating PivotTables, categorising data with conditional logic, and producing charts to communicate findings.

The project demonstrates the core skills required to analyse **retail and sales data**, identify trends and patterns, compare different customer or product groups, and present results in a clear and accessible format.
